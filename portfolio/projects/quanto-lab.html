Case study

QuantoLab

A live decision-support platform that turns questions about work, career and money into clearer, verifiable calculations.

Visit live product ↗GitHub ↗
ContextIndependent product · Live web platform · 2026
Areas
Product DesignUX/UIInformation ArchitectureProduct Strategy

Overview

Make the decision easier without hiding the calculation

QuantoLab is built for practical decisions around salary, CLT versus PJ, freelance pricing, projects and financial planning. The product currently brings together 28 tools and is structured so people can reach the main answer quickly, then inspect assumptions, formulas and sources when they want more depth.

My contribution

Product structure, interface and ongoing evolution

I structure the product direction, information architecture, interface system, UX writing and continuous evolution of the platform. The work focuses on reducing cognitive load, keeping calculations understandable and maintaining consistency as new tools are added.

Product principles

A system designed for trust, speed and clarity

01

Direct result

The main answer appears before technical detail, so the user can understand the decision first and investigate the calculation second.

02

Transparent calculation

Assumptions, composition, formulas and sources are exposed when applicable instead of being treated as a black box.

03

Low-friction use

No mandatory account is required, and optional references can stay in the browser when possible, keeping the experience lightweight.

Current state

QuantoLab is live at quantolab.com.br with 28 tools available across work, career and money. The product continues to evolve through interface refinement, new calculators and improvements to methodology, accessibility and content clarity.
