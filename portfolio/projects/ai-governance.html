AI Governance & Productization

A corporate innovation initiative focused on making AI applications more structured, reviewable and repeatable across real business contexts.

ContextCrescimentum · Corporate innovation · 2026
Areas
AI GovernancePrompt DesignTestingHuman ReviewDocumentation

Overview

What the project is solving

The work addresses a recurring challenge in corporate AI: moving from isolated experiments to applications that can be understood, tested, reviewed and documented. The initiative brings together prompt work, testing, quality criteria, human review and documentation.

My contribution

Where I contribute

I contribute to the structure behind these applications: evaluating AI tools, building and refining prompts, running tests, reviewing outputs and organizing the documentation needed to make the work more consistent and reusable.

Design focus

The parts of the work that matter most

01

Prompt design

Building and refining instructions with attention to purpose, context and expected output.

02

Quality & testing

Testing outputs against defined criteria instead of relying only on a single successful generation.

03

Human review & documentation

Keeping human evaluation and clear documentation as part of the solution, not as an afterthought.

Scope note

This case describes the structure of my work without disclosing internal prompts, proprietary workflows or confidential company information.
