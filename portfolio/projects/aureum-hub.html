Aureum Hub

An internal product that brings operational information, processes and decision-support resources into a single, more usable environment.

ContextAureum · Internal product · 2026
Areas
Product DesignInformation ArchitectureAutomationAI

Overview

What the project is solving

The Aureum Hub was conceived to reduce fragmentation in day-to-day operations. The product centralizes information, processes and supporting resources so the team can find what it needs with less friction and make decisions with better context.

My contribution

Where I contribute

My work spans Product Design, information architecture, experience structure, automation and AI. The focus is not on adding technology for its own sake, but on organizing the product around how information is found, understood and used.

Design focus

The parts of the work that matter most

01

Information architecture

Organizing content and operational resources into a structure that supports quick consultation.

02

Product experience

Connecting operational needs and user needs in a coherent internal product experience.

03

Automation & AI

Using automation and AI where they can support access to information and decision-making.

Scope note

Because this is an internal product, this case focuses on the product structure and my contribution rather than exposing sensitive operational details.
